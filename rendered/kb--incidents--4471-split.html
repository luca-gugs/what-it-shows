43 of 412 reports carry an intake tag that is not the problem they show. Intake tags come from the reporter's browser; the problem comes from the tenant, the channel and the booking form.

How to tell them apart

  • parser: the report shows a day/month swap (“Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July”), and it came in by app or email, or through a booking-v2 form. The shared parser read a day-first date month first.
  • hint: the same swap, but it came in through a booking-v3 form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The fix belongs in the form, so the parser fix does not touch it.
  • shift: no swap at all. The date moves by one day: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant. Swapping day and month never moves a date by one day, so the parser fix cannot close them. It looks like a time-zone conversion of the booking date.

The subject line does not tell them apart: most subjects turn up under more than one problem. Read the body, the tenant and the channel.

How the count was made

  1. Take the export of #4471 and keep one report per id. The ticket system exports pages that overlap, so some reports come twice.
  2. Place each report by its tenant (locale and booking form) and its channel, as causes.ts does. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not the cause.
  3. Take the reports the desk read as a one-day move out of parser. They are shift.

The numbers on this page are computed from the export when the page renders (#lib/kb/problems.ts), so they follow the data as the desk keeps merging.

What this changes

  • #4471 is not “known, one fix queued”. The queued fix covers parser. hint and shift need their own fixes and their own owners.
  • Merging on the subject alone (decision 0001) cannot keep these apart. That decision stays as it is; a new record should replace it.
